How to Play
Goal
Survive 10 floors of a cursed dungeon. Each floor, find the stairs to descend. Collect coins, avoid enemies, and reach the end with your HP intact.
Rolling & Moving
↑ ↓ ← →
Even Roll
Move in straight lines
↖ ↗ ↙ ↘
Odd Roll
Move diagonally
The die result is how many steps you take. You must use all of them.

HP & Death
You start with 10 HP and can hold up to 25 HP. If HP drops to 0 or below, you die — the floor resets with 10 HP and 0 coins, but your deaths are counted against your score.
Score
Coins × 10 + HP × 5 − Deaths × 100
